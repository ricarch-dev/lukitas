# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Economically active people who manage money across multiple currencies and use a mix of bank accounts, cash, and digital wallets. This includes multi-currency professionals and independent workers with variable income. The initial product is for individual use.

## Product Purpose

Lukitas helps people quickly record and understand their personal finances across currencies. It prioritizes consistent balances and explainable historical reports without depending on automatic bank aggregation.

## Brand Personality

Clear and trustworthy. Use straightforward Spanish, make the next action obvious, and keep financial information calm and legible.

## Anti-references

- Do not copy Rial's logo, QR code, visual assets, or exact interface.
- Avoid accounting jargon, opaque currency conversions, cluttered screens, and decorative controls that obscure common actions.
- Do not imply that Lukitas is a bank, custodian, or payment processor.

## Design Principles

- Explain financial state rather than decorate it; a balance or conversion must remain understandable.
- Keep common tasks quick and ask only for information required to complete them.
- Introduce secondary detail progressively instead of crowding the primary task.
- Preserve user trust through explicit, recoverable feedback and privacy-conscious handling of account access.
- Build an original Lukitas identity; use references for interaction inspiration, not as assets to replicate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Spanish for the auth entry experience, visible labels rather than placeholder-only instructions, readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, screen-reader names and states, and errors that explain how to recover. The project brief requires device accessibility QA; no formal WCAG conformance level has been selected.
