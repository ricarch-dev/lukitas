# Multicurrency Accounts

## Objective, problem, and why

Let one person track any number of manual accounts, including a Venezuelan bank account in VES and a separate USD account associated with that bank, then understand the selected accounts in VES, USD, or EUR without inventing exchange rates. Today the API supports multiple owned accounts with native balances, but mobile onboarding hardcodes USD and Inicio only displays accounts; it offers no post-onboarding account form, account-currency filter, or display-currency selection. The existing dashboard exposes one base-currency total, not totals for arbitrary selections or display currencies.

This correction unit edits only this tracker and its full Engram mirror at `odd/multicurrency-accounts/tasks` (project `lukitas`): no source edits or commit in this turn. Implementation authorization does not authorize remote execution, credential use, deployment, push, or PR. Preserve unrelated worktree edits, including `odd/tasks/home-dashboard-redesign.md`.
- Implement N user-owned manual accounts with a required label/name, native currency and non-negative opening balance. A national bank's VES account and its linked USD balance are **separate accounts** with distinct native ledgers, visibly grouped beneath the **same bank**. Use a persisted, owner-scoped bank grouping key/name if required to keep grouping durable across refresh and devices; do not rely on matching free-text labels alone. Grouping does not imply bank sync, automatic transfers, or a single dual-currency ledger. Keep each balance and ledger in its own native currency.
- Offer account-currency filters **Todas / Bolívares / Dólares** (all active accounts / VES accounts / USD accounts), updating both the visible account list and the selected-set balance. Filtering does not change account currency or silently include a hidden currency. Existing USDT accounts remain supported in the domain and in **Todas** where applicable, but USDT is not a filter choice; other supported account currencies must not disappear from **Todas**.
- Offer an independent **display currency** choice VES / USD / EUR for the selected-set balance. It changes only valuation/presentation, never account membership, native amounts, user base-currency preference, transactions, or historical facts. For a single-currency selection, show the native subtotal even if display conversion is unavailable; for mixed selections, show only proven converted contributions with an explicit partial label, omitted-currency detail, and rate-date/source warning. Never imply a complete total, substitute zero for a missing rate, or assume USD = EUR, VES = USD, or USDT = USD.
- Present official BCV-sourced USD/VES and EUR/VES quotations obtained through the Venezuelan DolarAPI endpoints using **`promedio` as an indicative valuation**, not an executable buy/sell price. Show provider source and update timestamp beside the rate and converted total. Mark stale quotes explicitly rather than passing them off as live; validate missing/failed/malformed responses. Derive USD/EUR only from two valid VES quotes with compatible dates under the canonical FX rules; **never mix quote dates** for a cross or invent a parity. New rates may value current balances but must never rewrite confirmed transaction/transfer FX snapshots.
- A static floating **+** on Inicio opens a real account-creation form (name/label, currency, opening balance, bank grouping for linked VES/USD accounts); it is not a decorative control and must remain reachable over scrolling content and on narrow/wide layouts. Keep the four existing native tabs and contextual Informes link. Preserve existing loading/error, empty, archived-account, flow, and partial-FX behavior; do not adopt Rial branding or fabricate bank connectivity.
- Reuse `AccountsService`/`AccountsController` (`GET/POST /accounts`), `accountDto`, `DashboardService`, `DashboardDto`, the canonical monetary-unit metadata in `packages/domain`, and existing FX/snapshot conventions. Put any shared projection in its proper API/contracts/domain owner; do not fork balance formulas into mobile. Exact decimals, strict typing, owned resources, idempotent writes, and small focused modules are required.
